Questions

Good to know.

Why custom software instead of an off-the-shelf tool?

Off-the-shelf tools make you change how you work and charge per user every month. Custom software follows your own process, has no per-user fee to me, and you own it. Projects start from $1,000.

Can I start from one of your existing systems?

Yes. That is the fastest and cheapest way. We pick the closest of the 17 systems, then I change it to fit your business.

Can you move my data from spreadsheets or an old system?

Yes. I import your existing customers, products, stock and history so you start with real data on day one.

Where is the software hosted?

On low-cost cloud hosting in your own account, or on your own server. I set it up and show you how backups work.

What happens after launch?

I fix any problems and add features as your business grows. Ongoing support is optional and agreed in writing.
