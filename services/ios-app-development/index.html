Questions

Good to know.

How much does an iPhone app cost?

It depends on the screens, the backend and whether you need subscriptions. I send a fixed written quote after we agree on the feature list. Apps start from $1,000, and a simple app can be ready in a few weeks.

Do you build Android apps too?

My main focus is native iOS. For apps that must be on both, I can build with Expo (React Native) or pair the iOS app with a web app that works on Android.

Will you publish the app for me?

Yes. I prepare the screenshots, description and keywords and submit it to Apple. The app is published under your own Apple developer account, so you own it.

Can you update or fix my existing app?

Yes. I can take over an existing Swift or SwiftUI app, fix crashes, update it for the latest iOS and add features.

Can the app connect to my website or system?

Yes. I build the API too, so the app and your web system share the same data.
