Questions

Good to know.

Which AI model should I use?

It depends on the task, the budget and how private the data is. I test two or three options on your real examples and show you the accuracy, speed and cost of each before we choose.

Is my data safe?

If the data is sensitive, I can use models that run on your own hardware, so nothing is sent outside. For cloud models, I use the providers' business APIs, which do not train on your data by default.

Can you add AI to my existing app?

Yes. Most AI features are an API plus a few screens, so they fit into an existing web or iOS app without a rebuild.

How do you stop the AI making things up?

By grounding answers in your own data, showing sources, checking outputs with rules, and testing on real cases before launch.

What does it cost?

Building an AI feature starts from $1,000, as a fixed quote. I also estimate the monthly model cost up front from your expected usage, and design it to stay low, for example with caching and smaller models where they are good enough.
