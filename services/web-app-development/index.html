Questions

Good to know.

How much does a web app cost?

It depends on the screens and features. After a short call or email about what you need, I send a written fixed quote with a list of every feature, so you know the full price before I start. Projects start from $1,000, and small apps and MVPs are usually a few weeks of work.

Can you work with my existing code?

Yes. I often join existing React, Next.js and Node projects. I read the code first and tell you honestly what is good, what is risky and what I would change.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code lives in your own GitHub account and hosting, and you get everything at handover.

How do we work together?

By email, chat and short calls. I share a working demo link as I build, so you can click through it and give feedback early.

Can you also build the mobile app?

Yes. I build native iOS apps in SwiftUI and can share one backend between the web app and the phone app.
