Looking for a MacWhisper alternative for translated video subtitles?

MacWhisper is one of the best Mac apps for turning speech into text. But if your job is subtitles for a video, in several languages, without sending the file anywhere, you may want a tool built for that. Here's an honest comparison. I made MLX Caption, so read it with that in mind.

Short answer

  • Pick MacWhisper if you mainly need transcripts, meeting notes or dictation into any app. It does those well, and it has a free version.
  • Pick MLX Caption if you need subtitles for a video in many languages, translated on your own Mac, ready to drop into YouTube or an editor.

Where MacWhisper is the better choice

If you record calls, write by voice, or just want a clean transcript of a podcast, MacWhisper has more for you. It records meetings, works as dictation in any app, and the free version covers basic transcription. MLX Caption does none of that. It's a subtitle tool.

Where MLX Caption fits better

Many languages in one pass. You pick the languages, and one run gives you subtitles in each of them, timed to the speech. For a course, a product video or a YouTube channel with viewers abroad, that's the whole job done at once.

Nothing leaves your Mac. Speech recognition, translation and scene descriptions all run on the Mac's own chip through Apple's MLX framework. After the first model download, it works offline. That matters for client videos, interviews and anything under an NDA.

A video file that's ready to share. Besides SRT and VTT, it exports an MP4 where every language is a subtitle track viewers can switch between, or with one language burned in.

A summary with chapters. You also get a short summary and chapter timestamps you can paste into a YouTube description.

Where MLX Caption falls short

  • It needs an Apple silicon Mac. Intel Macs aren't supported.
  • The first run downloads the models, which takes a while on a slow connection.
  • Machine translation is good but not perfect. For anything public, have a native speaker check the languages that matter most.
  • No meeting recording, dictation or speaker labels.
