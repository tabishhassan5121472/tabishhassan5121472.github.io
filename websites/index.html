How it works

Four steps, about a week.

Everything happens by email. You never need to install anything or learn a website builder.

  1. Free preview

    Send me your business name and website or Google listing. Within two or three days you get a link to a preview built with your own details.

  2. You decide

    Tell me what to change, or say no thanks. You pay nothing for the preview.

  3. I finish it

    Half the price to start. I add your pages, photos, menu or services, contact details and map, and you check every page.

  4. It goes live

    I put it on your own domain with HTTPS. You pay the second half when you are happy, and the site is yours.

Questions

Good to know.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. I build it before you pay anything, so you can see exactly what you would get. If it is not right for you, just say no thanks and that is the end of it.

Where are you based?

I am Tabish Hassan, a software developer in Rawalpindi, Pakistan. I work remotely with businesses in Australia, the United States and elsewhere, and reply to email within one working day.

I already have a website and a domain. Can I keep them?

Yes. The new site goes on your existing domain, and your email addresses keep working. If you do not have a domain yet, I help you buy one in your own name.

What does hosting cost after launch?

These sites are fast, simple pages, so they can run on free or very low-cost hosting. I help you set it up in your own account, so you are never tied to me.

How do I pay?

By international bank transfer or another method we agree by email. Half before I start the full build, and half when the site is live and you are happy with it.

Can you build something bigger, like online ordering, bookings or an app?

Yes. I am a software developer and also build business systems, web apps and iOS apps. You can see that work on my main site. Tell me what you need and I will quote it separately.
