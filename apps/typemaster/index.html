TypeMaster

Typing Practice

TypeMaster is a fast, focused typing-speed trainer for iPhone. Hit Drill, type the text on screen, and watch your live WPM and per-key accuracy tick in real time. No sign-up, no clutter — just a clean way to get faster and more accurate every day.

Unlike most typing apps, TypeMaster's drill field disables iOS auto-correct, auto-capitalization, and smart quotes while you type, so the keyboard never fights your test. Your speed score is your speed score.

Free, Every Day

  • Live WPM and accuracy on every drill
  • 5 typing drills per day (resets at midnight)
  • A 60-second Daily Challenge, once per day
  • 7-day rolling history of your drills
  • All 3 keyboard layouts — QWERTY, Dvorak, and Colemak
  • Personal best tracking for top WPM and top accuracy

TYPEMASTER PRO unlocks:

  • Unlimited drills — no daily cap
  • 100+ practice texts across prose, code, news, and dialogue
  • Per-Key Accuracy Heatmap — see exactly which keys are costing you accuracy, visualized on your chosen keyboard layout
  • Custom Text Drill — paste any paragraph, article, or script and drill it
  • Drill Difficulty picker — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert
  • Full unlimited history (the 7-day cap is removed)
  • Ad-free experience across the entire app

Perfect For

Students, writers, programmers, and anyone chasing a higher WPM or cleaner accuracy — whether you're practicing for 10 minutes a day or grinding toward 100+ WPM.

TypeMaster Pro is available as a monthly subscription ($2.99/month), an annual subscription ($29.99/year), or a one-time Lifetime purchase ($49.99) that unlocks everything forever with no recurring charge.
