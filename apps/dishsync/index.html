DishSync

Cooking Timers

Cooking a full dinner means juggling four timers in your head. DishSync runs them all for you — and makes every dish land on the table at the same time.

Multiple Named Timers

Run as many timers as your dinner needs, each with its own name, color and alarm. The dashboard shows every countdown at a glance.

Sync To Finish Together

Tell DishSync when you want to serve, and it tells you when to start each dish. No more cold sides next to a hot roast.

Preset Library

Common timings built in — pasta, rice, eggs, roast chicken and more. Save your own presets for the dishes you cook every week.

Recipe Planning

Break multi-step recipes into timed stages and let DishSync walk you through the cook.

Timer History

Look back at past cooking sessions to repeat what worked.

Premium

Unlock unlimited simultaneous timers, the full preset library, sync-to-finish planning and timer history with a monthly subscription.

Private By Design

No account and no cloud sync: your timers, recipes and cooking history are stored locally and are never uploaded to us. DishSync does show ads and report crashes, so advertising and diagnostic identifiers are shared with those providers.
