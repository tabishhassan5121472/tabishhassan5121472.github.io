CustodyLog Court Diary

CustodyLog is a private, one-sided custody documentation diary for separated parents. Log exchanges, incidents, expenses, and communications with dual timestamps, then export a court-ready PDF when you need it.

What You Can Log

  • Exchanges — on-time, late, or no-show with notes and photos
  • Incidents, missed visits, medical visits, school events, milestones
  • Shared expenses with reimbursement status
  • Parenting-time schedules and overnight counts
  • Amendment trail — entries lock after 24 hours; corrections become dated amendments

Court-ready Output

  • Formatted PDF report with entries, photos, expenses, and amendments
  • CSV export for spreadsheets (premium)
  • Local backup zip you control — restore merges, never deletes

Private By Design

  • No account and no cloud sync — your case data never leaves the device
  • Children, entries, expenses and photos are stored locally until you export them
  • Optional Face ID / passcode lock
  • Ads are shown to free users only, and never while you are writing an entry or

viewing a report — Premium removes them entirely

Custodylog Premium

  • Unlimited children and entries (free: 1 child, 25 entries)
  • Full-history court PDF without PREVIEW watermark
  • CSV export
  • Monthly $2.99, yearly $29.99, or lifetime $49.99

Perfect For

Parents documenting co-parenting exchanges, visitation issues, and shared expenses for family court, mediation, or personal records.
