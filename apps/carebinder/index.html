CareBinder

CareBinder is one calm, private place for everything about an aging parent.

If you are the one who keeps track — the appointments, the pill names, the insurance number the front desk always asks for, where the power of attorney is actually kept — CareBinder is the binder you would have made by hand.

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud, no sign-up, and nothing is ever uploaded.

One Binder Per Parent

  • Their profile: date of birth, blood type, allergies, conditions
  • Care contacts: the doctor, the specialist, the pharmacy, the insurance line, the

lawyer, family, aides — tap to call, email or map any of them

  • A medication list: what they take, the dose, when, and what it is for
  • Insurance and coverage: providers, policy and member numbers, renewal dates, and a

photo of the card

  • A document vault: photograph the POA, the will, the advance directive, an ID — and

note where the paper original is kept

  • Visit notes: a dated timeline of appointments, hospital stays, calls and everything

worth remembering

An Emergency Card You Can Share

Blood type, allergies, conditions, who to call, the physician, the insurance line — built automatically from what you have already entered, and shareable as a single image. Free and unlimited, always.

A Handoff Pdf For Whoever Steps In Next

A respite carer, a sibling flying in, a new aide. One tap builds a printable document with everything they need: who this person is, who to call, what they take and why, the coverage numbers, where the papers are, and what happened recently. Choose which sections to include.

Private By Design

  • Everything stays on this device — no account, no cloud, no network
  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID lock over the whole binder
  • When the lock is on, the binder hides itself in the app switcher

Works In Your Language

English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Urdu and Simplified Chinese.

What's Free

One parent profile, the emergency card and unlimited visit notes — plus 5 contacts, 5 medications, 5 insurance policies and 5 vault documents. The emergency card and visit notes stay free and unlimited whether or not you ever buy anything.

What Premium Adds

  • Unlimited contacts, medications and insurance policies
  • Unlimited documents in the vault
  • Multiple parent profiles
  • The handoff PDF with no preview watermark
  • CSV export of the whole binder

Choose a monthly or yearly plan, or unlock everything once with the lifetime purchase.

Please Read

CareBinder is an organizer and record-keeper. It is not medical, legal or financial advice, it does not remind about doses, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, pharmacist or lawyer. What you record here is your own record — always confirm medical details with the treating professionals.
