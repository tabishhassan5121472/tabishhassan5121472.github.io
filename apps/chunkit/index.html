ChunkIt

Tiny Steps

ChunkIt is a task-chunking app that breaks big, overwhelming goals into small, doable steps. Drag tasks into chunks, check them off, and watch your impossible project become manageable.

What You Can Do

  • Task Chunking — Create a big task (e.g., "Clean House") and split into N timed chunks (e.g., 5 × 10 min)
  • Auto-Suggest Chunk Sizes — Based on task complexity: Simple → 5 min, Medium → 15 min, Complex → 25 min
  • Built-in Focus Timer per Chunk — Countdown timer for the active chunk with pause/resume/skip controls
  • Pomodoro-Style Work/Break Flow — Automatic short break (5 min) between chunks, long break (15 min) after every 4 chunks
  • Chunk Completion Animation — Satisfying springy checkmark animation with haptic + confetti burst on chunk done
  • Task Templates — Preset task templates: Cleaning, Studying, Writing, Coding, Exercise, Cooking, Organizing (each with preloaded chunks)
  • Progress Bar — Visual bar and fraction label (e.g., "3 / 5 chunks done") per task
  • Daily Task Planner — Plan which chunked tasks to tackle today; drag-reorder within the day's queue
  • Streak Tracking — Consecutive days where all planned chunks are completed; flame badge with count
  • Statistics Dashboard — Tasks completed, total focus minutes, average chunk duration, daily/weekly completion rate, longest streak
  • No sign-up and no account — every chunk stays on your device.
