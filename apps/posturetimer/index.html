PostureTimer

PostureTimer is a private, time-based posture coach for desk workers. Set a reminder cadence and PostureTimer nudges you to stand, stretch, and reset on every interval inside your work-hours window. No camera, no motion sensors, no HealthKit - your session log lives entirely on your device.

Honest By Design

PostureTimer is TIME-BASED. It does not use the camera or sensors to detect your actual posture. Reminders fire on a schedule; staying upright is up to you.

Free

  • One reminder interval (30 min, 9am-5pm)
  • 5 starter desk stretches with step-by-step timers
  • Today's compliance ring + last-7-days mini chart
  • Manual timer start/stop
  • Full theme and notification controls

Posturetimer Premium

  • Focus + Stretch - work in a 25, 45 or 60 min focus block that ends in a guided sequence of 2-4 stretches across different body areas, logged to today's score
  • Custom Reminder Cadence - any interval 10-90 min, work-hours window, silent weekends
  • Full Stretch Library - 15+ desk-friendly exercises for neck, shoulders, back, wrists, legs
  • Posture Trend Analytics - weekly & monthly compliance, streaks, best-day
  • CSV Export - share your full session log
  • Siri Shortcuts - start breaks, log resets, check compliance hands-free
  • Ad-Free - removes all ads

Premium is available as a monthly ($2.99) or yearly ($29.99) auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase ($49.99). Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings. Lifetime is a single payment with no renewal.
