Bill Pulse is a calm, private bill and subscription tracker for people juggling rent, utilities, streaming services, gym memberships, software trials, and every other recurring charge. Add a bill in ten seconds and see exactly what is due, when, and how much you are spending each month.

No bank login. No account. No ads slowing you down. Just a clean, modern timeline of every recurring payment in your life.

Why People Love Bill Pulse

  • See your next seven days at a glance with the Pulse Bar timeline on the home screen
  • Add unlimited bills with category, amount, due date, and custom cadence
  • Get gentle local reminders before each bill is due so nothing slips through
  • Track payment history and mark bills paid with a single tap
  • Color-coded categories make rent, streaming, utilities, and memberships easy to scan
  • Beautiful light and dark mode with a signature violet pulse theme
  • 100% on-device storage — your financial data never leaves your iPhone

The Pro Upgrade

For power users tracking ten or more recurring payments, Bill Pulse Pro unlocks the features that turn a tracker into a money-saving habit:

  • Subscription Audit — automatically flags subscriptions that have been quietly renewing without use and shows your potential monthly and annual savings
  • Annual Forecast — a true twelve-month rollup of every recurring charge, broken down by category in a clean chart
  • Smart Reminders — pick your exact reminder cadence (three days before, one day before, morning of) and your preferred hour, not just a generic daily ping
  • CSV Export — send your full bill list and payment history to a spreadsheet or accountant in two taps
  • Unlimited Bills with no caps, ever
  • Ad-Free across the entire app

Bill Pulse Pro is $2.99 per month. Cancel anytime from Settings.

Privacy You Can Actually Trust

Most bill apps want your bank password. Bill Pulse does not. Everything is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no sign-in, no cloud account, no analytics SDK reading your spending. If you delete the app, your data is gone — really gone.

BUILT FOR MODERN iPHONE

  • Native SwiftUI design that feels at home on iOS 17 and later
  • Full dark mode with a custom electric violet palette
  • Haptic feedback on every meaningful action
  • iPad-optimized layouts
  • VoiceOver and Dynamic Type supported throughout

Who Is Bill Pulse For

  • Anyone trying to escape "subscription creep" without spreadsheets
  • Renters and homeowners tracking utilities, internet, and insurance
  • Freelancers monitoring software subscriptions and recurring vendor invoices
  • Households splitting streaming, music, and gaming services
  • People rebuilding their finances after a busy month or a big move
