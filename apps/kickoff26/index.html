KickOff26

Soccer Wall Chart

KickOff26 is your offline digital wall chart for the summer 2026 soccer tournament — all 12 groups, every fixture, and the full knockout tree, in your pocket. No account. No sign-up. No live-score server to break on the biggest match days.

Tap any match to enter a score and watch the group tables recalculate instantly with the proper tiebreakers (points, then head-to-head among level teams, then goal difference and goals for). When the groups finish, the best third-placed teams rank automatically and the knockout bracket from the Round of 32 to the Final fills itself in — drawn as a real wall-chart tree with connector lines — and you can tap to override any slot.

Wall Chart, Digitized

  • All 12 groups with live-computing standings
  • Full schedule grouped by day — opens at today automatically
  • Manual score entry with steppers and haptics
  • Round of 32 → Final bracket tree that auto-populates and stays editable

On Your Time

  • Every kickoff shown in your local time AND the stadium's local time
  • Midnight-crossing kickoffs show the day, so "12:00 AM" is never ambiguous
  • Kickoff reminders with a 15 / 30 / 60-minute lead — all local, no push server
  • Spoiler-free mode hides scores behind a tap-to-reveal so a glance never spoils a match
  • Pick your team and get their next match front and center with a one-tap reminder

Your Bracket, Your Way

  • Build full prediction brackets — group scores AND knockout picks — separate from the actual results
  • Reset a single stage or re-seed your groups from reality without wiping your bracket
  • Compare your picks against reality with the scored picks-vs-actual overlay (Pro)

KickOff26 Pro

  • Home-screen widgets: Next Kickoff countdown and your group's live table
  • Unlimited what-if prediction brackets
  • Picks-vs-actual overlay with a running bracket score
  • Share cards for the group chat
  • Chart themes: Daylight, Retro Chalk and Heatmap

Pro is a $2.99/month auto-renewable subscription with a 7-day free trial — cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

KickOff26 is an independent, unofficial wall chart. Team names, venues, and dates are presented as factual schedule information and it is not affiliated with any official tournament organizer.

Subscription Information

KickOff26 Pro is an auto-renewable subscription billed at $2.99/month after a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
