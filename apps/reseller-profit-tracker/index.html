Reseller Profit Tracker

Reseller Profit Tracker tells you what a flip ACTUALLY made — after the marketplace fee, after shipping, after everything.

Log an item the moment you find it. Snap a photo at the counter, enter what you paid, and follow it through Sourced → Listed → Sold. When it sells, pick the marketplace and the app applies that platform's fee, subtracts your shipping, adds what the buyer paid, and shows you the net and the ROI. No mental arithmetic at the end of the month.

Free Marketplace Fee Calculator

Standing in a shop wondering whether it's worth it? Open the calculator, enter the price you'd sell at and what you'd pay, and see the fee and the net before you buy. Free, unlimited, and it works without adding an item. Fourteen marketplace presets are built in — eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, OLX, Etsy, Grailed, Whatnot, Carousell, Shpock, Gumtree and Local/Cash — and every one of them is editable, because fee schedules change.

Know Your Real Margin

  • Net profit per sale after fees, shipping paid, shipping charged and any other cost
  • ROI as a percentage, and how many days the item took to sell
  • A monthly net-profit chart across the last six months
  • Revenue, items sold and inventory-at-cost for the current month at a glance
  • Returns reverse a sale's profit and send the item back to your inventory

Kill The Death Pile

The stuff you bought and never listed is dead cash on a shelf. The app counts it, adds up exactly how much money is sitting there, and groups your still-held stock by how long it has been held — 30, 60 and 90+ days — so you know what to discount before it costs you anything more.

Multi-platform Listings, No Ghost Inventory

List one item on three marketplaces and track all three. The moment you record the sale, the other listings close automatically — no more selling something twice.

Sourcing Trips

Group a whole haul under one trip, add your fuel, entry and parking costs, and see whether that flea market actually paid. It even shows how much of the trip is still tied up in unsold stock.

Reports You Can Hand Over

Per-item profit and loss for this month, this year or all time, exported as CSV or PDF with a totals row. Share it straight to your accountant, your spreadsheet or your files.

Works Anywhere

Everything is stored on your device. No account, no sign-up, no internet connection required, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Choose from 48 currencies. Available in 14 languages.

Premium

The free tier covers 30 active items and 3 report exports. Premium unlocks unlimited inventory, unlimited CSV and PDF exports, and no more upgrade prompts — monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

Record-keeping only — not tax or accounting advice.
