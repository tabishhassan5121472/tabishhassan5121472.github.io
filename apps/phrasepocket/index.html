PhrasePocket

Travel Phrases

PhrasePocket is a privacy-first, offline travel phrasebook. Pick your destination's language, tap a category, tap a phrase — and hear it spoken aloud in Apple's native locale voice. The entire phrase corpus ships inside the app, so it works in airplane mode, on a train with no signal, or roaming abroad without a data plan. No account, no cloud sync, no analytics — the only thing PhrasePocket remembers is the phrases you star.

How It Works

  • Choose your travel language
  • Tap a category — Greetings, Food, Directions, Emergency, Transport, Accommodation, and more
  • Tap a phrase to hear it spoken in a real native-locale voice
  • Star your most-used phrases so they're one tap away in Favorites

Free, No Paywall On Launch

  • 50 essential travel phrases across every category
  • 3 languages: English, Spanish, French
  • Native text-to-speech playback in all 3 free languages
  • Unlimited favorites
  • Full offline mode — no internet required

Phrasepocket Premium

  • 9 additional languages with native-locale speech: German, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Russian, Turkish, Urdu, and Chinese
  • 20+ deeper phrases per category — "extra towel," "platform number," "fire department," and more for real situations beyond the basics
  • Pronunciation Coach — a guided slow-normal-fast drill with syllable highlighting, so you can practice saying a phrase before you need it, not just hear it once
  • Ad-free experience

Premium is for anyone going further than Western Europe — one correctly pronounced phrase in Tokyo, Istanbul, or Mumbai pays for the subscription in a single interaction.
