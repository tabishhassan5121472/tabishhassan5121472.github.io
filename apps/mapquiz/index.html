MapQuiz

Geography Quiz

MapQuiz turns the world map into a daily habit. Test yourself on countries, capitals, flags, continents, and population across 108 countries spanning every region on Earth — no account, no sign-up, just open the app and play.

Free, Right Now

  • Country Quiz — name the country a capital city belongs to
  • Capital Quiz — name the capital city of a country
  • Flag Quiz — identify the country from its flag
  • Continent Quiz — place a country on the correct continent
  • Region Focus packs for World, Africa, and Europe — drill one part of the map at a time
  • Review My Misses — every question you get wrong is saved, so you can drill your misses until they stick

Mapquiz Premium

  • Population Quiz — pick the most populous country out of four
  • Daily Challenge — 10 mixed questions every day, with your best streak on the Home screen
  • Four more Region Focus packs: Asia, Americas, Oceania, and Middle East
  • Learn Mode — search and browse country cards with capital, population, area, language, and currency for all 108 countries
  • Full Statistics — accuracy by quiz type, best score, and your recent rounds
  • Ad-free — no banners, no interstitials while Premium is active

Three difficulty levels set the length of a round and the clock: Easy gives you 20 seconds per question, Medium 15, Hard 10. On Hard, the wrong answers come from the same continent as the right one, so you actually have to know the map.

MapQuiz Premium is available as a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase:
