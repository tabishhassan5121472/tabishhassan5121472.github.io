Staff Attendance

Time Clock

Staff Attendance turns one phone into a time clock for your whole team.

Leave it on the counter. Each person taps their own name to clock in and out — nobody has to install anything, and nobody needs an account. Late arrivals are flagged automatically against the shift you set, overtime is counted past shift end, and short days become half days on their own.

When payday comes, the register is already payroll.

A Shared Device, Not An App Per Person

  • Big, tappable name cards — readable across a counter
  • Clock in and out with one tap
  • Live clock and a running count of who is in, finished, and still to arrive
  • Owner side locked behind a 4-digit PIN

Shifts That Match How You Actually Work

  • Any start and end time, including night shifts that cross midnight
  • Unpaid break deducted from paid hours
  • Grace period before someone counts as late
  • Six-day weeks, five-day weeks, or a different shift for each weekday
  • More than one shift per person per day

Payroll That Adds Up

  • Hourly or monthly pay per employee
  • Overtime at your own multiplier
  • Optional deduction for late arrivals — off by default
  • Company holidays removed from the working-day count, and never counted as absences
  • Anyone who joined mid-month is prorated, not docked
  • Bonuses, advances, allowances and deductions as separate line items
  • Payslips you can print or share as PDF

Leave And Absence

  • Sick, casual, annual and unpaid leave
  • Approve, reject, or set a decision back to pending
  • Yearly entitlements per person, with a warning when a request goes over
  • Only approved unpaid leave is deducted — paid leave costs your team nothing

On-site Verification, If You Want It

  • Optional selfie on clock-in
  • Optional work-site check using GPS, with a radius you choose
  • A supervisor can allow an off-site clock-in with the owner PIN — and it is recorded as one
  • All of this is off by default

Reports

  • Attendance by day, by person, or across any date range
  • Status breakdown and attendance-rate charts
  • Export to CSV for your accountant, or PDF to print

Your Data Stays On Your Phone

Everything is stored on the device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Back up to a file whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone — restoring adds to what is there and never deletes anything.
