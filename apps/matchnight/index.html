MatchNight

Party Planner

MatchNight turns any game day into an easy, organized watch party — and it works entirely offline with no account to create.

Whether you're hosting a match, an NFL Sunday, a season finale, or a birthday gathering, MatchNight gives you one clean kit to run the whole evening.

Plan Any Event, Any Time

  • Build a party around a bundled Summer 2026 fixture OR a fully custom event of your own.
  • Every kickoff and start time shows in YOUR local timezone — no more confused guests doing time-zone math.
  • Set how early guests should arrive and get a reminder before the party starts.

The Potluck Bring-board

  • Add snacks, drinks, mains, desserts and supplies, sorted by category.
  • Let each guest claim what they'll bring, with clear claimed / unclaimed states so nothing is forgotten — and no two people show up with the same chips.
  • Tap the Ideas button for ready-made snack & drink packs.

The Fun Team Draw

  • Randomly assign each guest a side to cheer for — purely for fun and bragging rights.
  • No money, no wagering, no prizes, ever. Just friendly game-day rivalry.
  • Assign sides manually, or upgrade for a confetti-style animated auto-draw.

Share A Finished Card

  • Export a polished invite card or results card as an image and drop it straight into your group chat.
  • Guests don't need the app or an account — sharing is just a picture and a message.

Private By Design

  • Everything lives on your device. No sign-up, no server, no ads, no tracking.

MATCHNIGHT PRO ($2.99/month, 7-day free trial)

  • Unlimited simultaneous parties — host the whole tournament or every game-day season.
  • Animated auto-draw with a confetti reveal.
  • 6 invite-card & party themes (free includes 2).
  • Premium snack & drink idea packs — BBQ, taco bar, sweet tooth and more.
  • Points-for-fun leaderboard history across your parties.

The free version lets you run one full party end to end — guest list, potluck board, team draw, reminders and invite-card sharing — so you can plan a complete game day before deciding to upgrade.
