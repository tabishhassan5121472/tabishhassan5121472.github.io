RetainerLedger

RetainerLedger is the privacy-first invoicing app built for freelancers, consultants, and small service businesses who get paid for their time. No account, no cloud sync, no analytics — your clients, invoices, and receivables live on your device, period.

Most invoicing apps want a credit card, an email, and a tour through a full accounting suite before you can send a single PDF. RetainerLedger takes the opposite approach: open the app, add a client, send a polished invoice in under a minute. Track who paid, who's late, and what's due next — all from one calm, focused screen.

The home Invoice Pulse hero is your money command center. See total outstanding dollars, open invoice count, the age of your oldest unpaid invoice, and a per-client micro-bar of who owes what — all at a glance, every time you open the app. No more guessing which client to nudge.

Features

  • Unlimited invoices, clients, and line items — no artificial limits on the free tier
  • Invoice Pulse hero — outstanding total, open count, oldest-days, per-client bars
  • Five status states — Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue, Cancelled — with automatic aging
  • Standard PDF invoice template (Header + line items + totals) — share via Mail, Messages, AirDrop
  • Five-step client records — name, company, email, phone, billing address
  • Search and filter by client, status, or date range
  • Five-tab navigation — Invoices, Clients, Pulse, Settings, Premium
  • Local currency formatting based on your device locale
  • Local-only storage with SwiftData — your data never leaves your iPhone

PREMIUM (RetainerLedger Pro — $2.99/month)

  • Recurring Invoice Schedules — pick a client, template, and cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly). RetainerLedger auto-creates the next draft and notifies you when it's due. Solves the monthly-retainer-copy-paste problem outright.
  • Branded PDF Templates — Classic, Modern, and Minimal layouts with custom footer copy and accent striping.
  • 30/60/90 Aging Report — bucketed receivables view so you know exactly how stale your AR is and which clients to chase first.
  • CSV Export — every invoice and every line item in a single file. Drop into a spreadsheet or hand to your CPA at tax time.
  • Ad-free — banners and interstitials disappear immediately.

Premium unlocks the four features freelancers actually pay $15–$20/month for at FreshBooks, Invoice2go, or Bookipi — without ever asking for an account.
