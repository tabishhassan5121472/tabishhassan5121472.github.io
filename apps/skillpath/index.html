SkillPath

Skill Roadmap

SkillPath turns "I want to learn X" into a real roadmap. Set a skill, give it a target in hours, break it into milestones, and log every practice session — the home screen tracks your progress as a chartreuse ring so you always know how close you are to mastery. Everything lives on your device: no account, no cloud, no login wall.

How It Works

  • Add a skill (a language, an instrument, a craft — anything) and set a target in hours
  • Break it into milestones you write yourself, like a curriculum
  • Log each practice session with minutes, notes, and a 1–5 rating
  • Watch the progress ring fill as your practice hours add up
  • Keep a daily streak going with a reminder notification

Free

  • Up to 2 active skills, with unlimited milestones and sessions inside each
  • Daily practice log with minutes, notes, and rating
  • 30-day rolling history per skill
  • Streak counter and this-week minutes on the home screen
  • Daily reminder notification
  • Four built-in practice templates (15 / 30 / 45 / 60-minute session structures)

Skillpath Premium

  • Unlimited skills and unlimited milestones
  • Unlimited custom practice templates, with the option to pin one to a specific skill
  • Advanced statistics: weekly practice chart, hours-by-skill breakdown, 12-month heatmap, and skill velocity trend
  • Flexible reminder patterns — daily, weekdays only, or custom days of the week
  • CSV export of every practice log
  • Ad-free

Premium is available as a $2.99 monthly subscription, a $29.99 yearly subscription, or a $49.99 one-time lifetime purchase — pick whichever fits how you learn. All plans unlock the same features; lifetime is a single payment with no recurring charge.

Perfect For

Anyone building a real skill instead of ticking a habit box — language learners tracking conversation hours, musicians logging practice blocks, anyone working toward a certification or craft one session at a time. SkillPath is built for iPhone and iPad.

No. SkillPath works without an account or sign-in.
