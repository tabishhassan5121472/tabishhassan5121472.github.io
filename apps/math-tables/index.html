Math Tables

Learn & Practice

Math Tables turns times-table practice into something you actually look forward to. Hear each table read aloud, drill it as a quiz, race the clock, then watch your accuracy climb week after week.

What You Can Do

  • Browse the tables — every table from 1 to 100, each one read aloud so numbers get heard as well as seen
  • Take a quiz — 20 multiplication questions at four difficulty levels
  • Fix your Tricky Facts — every fact you miss in a quiz or challenge is collected for you. Practise them with big answer buttons and read-aloud, and clear each one by getting it right three times in a row
  • Flip flashcards — tap a card to see the answer, then mark it known or needs practice
  • Race the clock — a timed challenge with the countdown tuned to each difficulty
  • Keep a daily streak — a fresh 10-question challenge every day, rotating through addition, subtraction, multiplication and division
  • See real progress — accuracy charts, your recent quiz history, a Mastery Map and achievements to earn
  • Practise on your terms — three pronunciation styles, three reading speeds, light or dark theme, and a daily practice reminder

Free Includes

Tables 1 to 12, quizzes and flashcards on Easy and Normal, Tricky Facts, achievements and read-aloud.
