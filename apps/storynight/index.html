StoryNight

Bedtime Stories

StoryNight turns bedtime into a cozy ritual. Choose a story, add your child's name and their favourite things, and it becomes their story — read on screen or read aloud to them. Everything stays on your device, and no account is needed.

21 Original Bedtime Stories

Every story is written for this app and runs a full 12 pages, across seven shelves: Adventure, Fairy Tale, Space, Ocean, Sleepy Time, Animals and Magic. Each one is written to wind down — the last pages are quiet, and every story ends on a goodnight.

Made Theirs

Add your child's name, their best friend, the animal they love and their favourite colour. Save a profile once and every story fills itself in. The artwork even picks up their favourite colour.

Read Aloud

Tap play and StoryNight reads the story out loud, turning its own pages and highlighting each word as it goes, so a child can follow along. Adjust the reading speed to suit them.

Illustrated Throughout

Every page has its own hand-built night scene — moonlit hills, deep forests, coral reefs, quiet bedrooms — drawn right in the app, so it all works offline.

What's Free

Seven stories, one on every shelf, free to read with no time limit. Read-aloud narration, reading-speed control, unlimited saved stories, favourites, and up to two child profiles.

Storynight Premium

  • All 21 stories — unlocks the 14 premium ones
  • Choose the narration voice from any voice on your device
  • Unlimited child profiles
  • No ads anywhere in the app

Perfect For

Parents and caregivers who want short, gentle bedtime stories without another streaming subscription. No account, no sign-up, no chat, and nothing about your child leaves the device.

Premium is available monthly or yearly. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, and can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple ID Subscriptions settings.
