QuizBuilder

Custom Quiz Make

QuizBuilder turns anything you need to memorise into a quiz you can actually practise. Write your own questions, run timed practice, and watch your scores climb — no account, no cloud, no sign-up.

Built for students, language learners, and anyone studying for an exam or a certification.

What You Can Do

  • Daily Drill — a short set every day, drawn from across your quizzes and weighted toward previously incorrect answers. Build a streak. Free, no subscription.
  • Build your own quizzes — multiple choice, true/false, or written answer
  • Add an explanation to any question, so a wrong answer teaches you something
  • Practise as often as you like, with instant scoring and correct/incorrect feedback on every question
  • Shuffle questions each run, and set how many questions a run should serve
  • Turn on a per-question timer (15–90 seconds) when you want exam-style pressure
  • Track your best score on every quiz
  • Organise quizzes by category and search your whole library
  • Import quizzes from plain text, JSON, or CSV — just paste and go
  • Quiz Friends — swap codes with someone and send a quiz straight to them. Sharing is off until you turn it on, you choose who to accept, and nothing joins your library until you tap Save.
  • Set a daily practice reminder
  • No account required. Your quizzes live on your device — the only thing that ever leaves it is a quiz you choose to send to a friend.
  • Full dark mode, and your quizzes work offline once they're on your device

QUIZBUILDER PRO — $2.99/month:

  • Full statistics — progress charts and your complete practice history
  • Scheduled practice — pick the days and time, get reminded to run a specific quiz
  • Export & share — send any quiz out as shareable text, JSON, or CSV. Anyone else with QuizBuilder can paste it straight in, so a study group can share one question set.
  • Long practice runs — play more than 30 questions in a single run, up to the whole quiz
  • Ad-free experience

The free version is genuinely usable: unlimited quizzes, unlimited questions, all three question types, as many practice runs as you like (up to 30 questions per run), the Daily Drill with its streak, Quiz Friends, and a backup file you can keep and restore any time. Pro adds longer runs, progress tracking, scheduling, export, and removes ads.

Perfect For

Exam prep, vocabulary drills, certification study, classroom revision, or quizzing yourself on anything you keep forgetting. Designed for iPhone and iPad.
