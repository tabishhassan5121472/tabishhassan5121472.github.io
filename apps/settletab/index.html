SettleTab

Split Bills

Who paid for the rideshare? Whose turn was it to grab the groceries? How much does Sam still owe from the cabin trip?

SettleTab is the calmest way to split a bill with the people in your life. Open a tab for the trip, the dinner, or the shared apartment, drop expenses in as they happen, and let SettleTab work out the minimum settlements automatically — who pays who, exactly how much, and when.

No bank login. No email signup. No cloud account quietly syncing your dinner receipts to a server you have never heard of. Just a beautiful, private, on-device tracker that lives on your iPhone and asks nothing of you.

What Settletab Is Great For

  • Splitting rent, utilities, internet, and groceries with roommates
  • Tracking who paid for what on a weekend trip or vacation
  • Running a dinner tab when one person picks up the check
  • Sharing the cost of gifts, parties, or group purchases
  • Settling up at the end of the month without spreadsheet gymnastics

How It Works

1. Open a tab — name the group, add the members. 2. Add expenses — pick who paid, choose equal, exact, or percentage split. 3. See the settlement view — SettleTab figures out the fewest payments that close the tab. 4. Tap "Mark Settled" once everyone has paid up. Done.

Why People Love Settletab

  • Tab Pulse home screen shows total in flight, open settlements, and a settled-this-month tally across every group at once
  • Three split modes built in — equal, exact amount, and percentage
  • Smart minimum-transfer settlement means the fewest possible payments to close out a group
  • Unlimited groups, members, and expenses, with no artificial caps on the free tier
  • Beautiful light and dark mode in a signature sunset rose theme
  • iPad-optimized layouts and VoiceOver support throughout
  • 100 percent on-device storage with SwiftData — nothing leaves your phone

The Pro Upgrade

For people running roommate splits month after month, or a frequent-trip group with messy reimbursements, SettleTab Pro adds the features that turn a tracker into a real time-saver:

  • Smart Tip and Tax Defaults — set a per-app default tip and tax percentage and let SettleTab pre-fill them on every new expense, with a one-tap "split tip evenly" toggle.
  • Settlement Reminders — schedule a friendly local notification for any pending settlement. Pick the lead time and the hour. SettleTab will quietly remind you that Sam still owes you.
  • One-Tap CSV Export — send every group's settlements to a spreadsheet or your accountant as a single CSV in two taps.
  • Ad-Free — remove the small banner and any house ads from the entire app.

SettleTab Pro is $2.99 per month. Cancel anytime from Settings.

Privacy You Can Actually Trust

SettleTab never asks for your email, an account, or your bank. Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no sign-in, no cloud account, no behavioral analytics. If you delete the app, your data is gone — really gone.

Who Is Settletab For

  • Roommates who do not want to fight about the utility bill anymore
  • Couples planning a trip with friends
  • Frequent travelers running-tab groups
  • Anyone tired of "Venmo me when you get a chance" turning into never

Stop doing mental math at the table. Stop hunting through screenshots of receipts. Open a tab in SettleTab and let the app handle the awkward part.
