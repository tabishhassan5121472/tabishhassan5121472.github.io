CurbNotes

Street Reports

CurbNotes is a private logbook for the things you notice on your block: the streetlight that has been out for a week, a car that keeps blocking the hydrant, late-night noise, a broken fence, a fox in the gardens. Write it down while it is fresh, see where it happened, and follow it until it is fixed.

Log An Observation In Seconds

  • Give it a title and a description of what you saw
  • Choose a category: Safety, Noise, Property, Parking, Wildlife, Community, Maintenance or Other
  • Set a severity: Informational, Low, Moderate or Urgent
  • Your current location and street address are attached automatically
  • Add extra notes for anything you want to remember later

See Your Block On A Map

Every observation appears as a pin at the spot where you logged it. Pins show the category icon and are coloured by severity, so urgent issues stand out at a glance.

Follow Each Issue To The End

  • Browse all your observations and search them by title
  • Open any entry to review the details and mark it Resolved once it is dealt with
  • The home screen keeps your open issues and most recent entries in front of you

Premium: Reports And Statistics

  • Reports - select the observations that belong together, give the report a title and summary notes, and tag who it is for (HOA, police, city or other), then share it by Mail or Messages
  • Statistics - see your total and resolved counts and a chart of observations by category, so you can tell which problems keep coming back

Optional Account Backup

You never need an account to use CurbNotes. If you want a safety net, sign in with Apple or Google in Settings to back up your observations and reports, then restore them on a new phone with the same account. You can sign out or delete your account at any time.

More

  • Optional daily evening reminder to log anything you noticed
  • Light, dark or system appearance
  • Contact and feedback straight from Settings

CurbNotes is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not contact emergency services or share anything with your neighbours. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Subscription

CurbNotes Premium is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID account settings after purchase.
