Every morning, a new case lands on your desk: a theft at the manor, a disappearance aboard the night train, a gala gone wrong. Suspects have alibis. Clues don't add up — until they do. Work the deduction grid, cross-reference the evidence, and accuse the culprit.

Why Detectives Love Caseaday

  • PURE LOGIC, GUARANTEED — every case is machine-verified to have exactly one solution reachable by deduction alone. There is always a next step. You will never have to guess.
  • ONE CASE A DAY — a 5-minute ritual with a solve streak worth protecting. Rookie starts the week, Chief closes it on Sunday.
  • A REAL DEDUCTION GRID — mark X and checkmarks across Suspect × Location, Suspect × Item, and (on Chief days) Suspect × Time boards, exactly like classic logic-grid puzzles.
  • INSPECTOR'S NUDGE — a hint that flags a wrong mark or reveals a safe next deduction. Never a spoiler.
  • NO TIMERS, NO ENERGY — play at your own pace. Ads never interrupt an active case.
  • SPOILER-FREE SHARING — share your time, nudges, and accuracy without revealing the culprit.
  • DETECTIVE STATS — streaks, solve-time trends, no-hint solves, and a rank that climbs from Trainee to Superintendent.
  • MORNING REMINDER — one optional notification when the new case is on your desk.

The Detective's Kit

Earn Magnifiers from the daily video, streak milestones, and perfect weeks, or top up with optional packs. Spend them on extra nudges mid-case, magnifier skins, verdict stamps, Case Files packs that permanently unlock past dailies, and a once-per-streak repair for a missed day.

Optional Account Backup

Cases are generated on your device and play fully without an account. If you like, sign in with Apple or Google in Settings to back up your streaks, stats, and Kit, and restore them on a new device. You can delete the account from Settings at any time.

DETECTIVE PRO (optional)

  • The full case archive — reopen every mystery ever published
  • Practice Desk — extra Rookie, Inspector, and Chief cases anytime
  • Unlimited Inspector's Nudges
  • Auto error-check on your pencil marks
  • 15 Magnifiers a month plus every desk theme while subscribed
  • No ads

Detective Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. A separate one-time Remove Ads purchase is also offered. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.
