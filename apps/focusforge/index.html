FocusForge

Deep Work Timer

FocusForge is a Pomodoro timer built for deep work. Start a session, tag it, and let the timer carry you through focus and breaks without you having to think about it. Come back later to see exactly where your focused hours actually went.

No account and no cloud sync: your sessions, streaks and stats stay on your device.

What You Can Do

  • Run a focus session — a calm circular timer that moves between work and break on its own
  • Tag what you worked on — Study, Work, Creative, Exercise, Reading or Other, each with its own colour
  • Pick your rhythm — classic Pomodoro (25/5), Long Focus (50/10), Quick Burst (15/3), or build your own
  • Set a daily goal — choose a target and watch the progress ring fill
  • Keep a streak — consecutive focused days, with a flame once you get going
  • Look back — history grouped by day and filterable by category
  • See the shape of your week — weekly bars, a category breakdown, and a 30-day trend (Premium)
  • Mask the noise — Focus Sounds layers rainfall, brown noise, ocean waves and a crackling fireplace, each with its own volume slider, plus four quick mixes
  • Get a nudge — an optional daily reminder at a time you choose

Free Includes

Unlimited focus sessions, all six categories, the three standard presets, your daily goal and streak, full session history, today's stats on the Home screen, and the daily reminder. A Focus Sounds session or a new custom preset is yours after one short video.

Premium Unlocks

  • The full Statistics tab — weekly bars, category breakdown and a 30-day trend
  • Custom timer presets — as many as you like, no video
  • Focus Sounds without the video — rainfall, brown noise, ocean waves and fireplace
  • No ads — no banners and no full-screen ad breaks

Perfect For

Students, developers, writers, researchers and remote workers — anyone using the Pomodoro Technique to protect their attention.
