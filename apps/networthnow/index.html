NetWorthNow

NetWorthNow is a private wealth tracker that turns what you own and what you owe into a single trend line you can watch climb. No bank logins. No email signup. Your assets, debts and net-worth history live on your device.

Add your assets — cash, investments, property, vehicles, retirement, crypto, the random things only you remember — and the debts that sit against them. Tap one button to snapshot your net worth today, and NetWorthNow does the rest: a 30-day sparkline, a delta arrow, and a live breakdown of where your money actually lives.

What You Can Do

  • Net Worth Pulse — A clean home hero with current net worth, 30-day change, and a sparkline drawn from every snapshot you've taken. The first thing you see is the only thing that matters.
  • Unlimited Assets and Debts — No artificial caps. Categorize each entry (cash, investments, property, vehicle, retirement, crypto, other) and watch the totals roll up automatically.
  • Debt Payoff Planner — Compare Snowball (smallest balance first) and Avalanche (highest interest first) side by side from your own balances, interest rates and minimum payments. See each strategy's debt-free date, total interest and the order your debts clear, then adjust the extra amount you can pay each month. Find it on the Debts tab under Plan My Payoff.
  • Daily Auto-Snapshot — Turn it on once. The first time you open NetWorthNow each day, your net worth is recorded automatically. The trend line keeps drawing even when life gets busy.
  • Allocation Pulse — Set a target percentage per category and NetWorthNow flags drift in percentage points. Color-coded so you know at a glance whether your mix is on plan or sliding.
  • One-File CSV Export — Assets, debts, and every snapshot in a single share-sheet-ready file. Hand it to your accountant or open it in any spreadsheet.
  • Full History — Premium shows every snapshot, with up to 10 years kept. Without Premium, the History screen shows the last 90 days and older snapshots stay safely on your device.
  • Local Currency — Reads from your device. No currency hunting, no setup.
  • Private by design — no account required and no cloud sync. Your wealth is nobody's business but yours.

NETWORTHNOW PREMIUM (monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime):

  • Debt Payoff Planner (build a plan whenever you like; without Premium, watch a short ad to build one plan)
  • Daily Auto-Snapshot (the trend line draws itself)
  • Custom Allocation Targets (drift in percentage points vs your goals)
  • Full History (every snapshot, up to 10 years)
  • CSV Export (assets + debts + snapshots in one file)
  • Ad-Free Experience

Perfect For

Anyone serious about a long-term net-worth ledger who would rather not hand a bank login over to a third-party app. Investors, FIRE planners, people paying down debt, real-estate owners, freelancers tracking equipment value, and anyone who wants a calm, private place to watch their wealth climb over the years.

Debt payoff plans are illustrations based on the numbers you enter, not financial advice.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID Subscriptions settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that never renews.
