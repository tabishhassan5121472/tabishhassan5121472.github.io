Why Taxpot

Most tax apps are seasonal, intimidating, and demand you connect your bank and create yet another login. TaxPot flips that. Open the app, tap a button, and your year-to-date tax estimate updates in real time. It's the ledger a freelancer actually wants: fast to log, honest about what you owe, and quiet about your data.

Features You'll Use Every Week

YTD Tax Pulse Hero A single signature dashboard card on the Home tab pairs year-to-date income, year-to-date deductibles, your estimated YTD federal tax, and a live countdown to April 15. One glance — you know where you stand.

Unlimited Income & Deductions Log every 1099 payment and every receipt. No caps, no upsell to log more. Fourteen Schedule C-style categories built in — advertising, car and vehicle, insurance, legal and professional, office, rent, supplies, travel, meals, utilities, phone, software, education, and other.

Quarterly Estimator Self-employment tax (15.3 percent on 92.35 percent of net), federal progressive brackets, and a per-quarter target so you can move the right amount into your tax-savings account each April, June, September, and January.

Quarterly Payments Tracker Log each estimated-tax payment you send against its quarter and see what you've paid next to TaxPot's estimate. Every quarter shows its federal due date, unpaid quarters count down the days left, and optional reminders arrive a week before and on the morning of any quarter you haven't paid yet.

Filing Profile Save your filing status (single, married filing jointly, head of household), state-tax tier, and retirement contributions in Settings — the Tax Scenario Calculator starts from them.

Power Up With Premium

Deduction Audit A pre-CPA hygiene pass over every entry. Flags missing descriptions, future-dated rows, suspiciously round large amounts, "Other"-category mis-files, and quarter mismatches. The kind of cleanup paid bookkeepers do manually.

Tax Scenario Calculator Toggle filing status, state-tax tier, and retirement contribution and watch the estimate change. See what filing jointly, moving to a no-tax state, or maxing a SEP-IRA would do to your bill. Pure on-device math — no waiting.

CSV Ledger Export Every income and deduction row, year-tagged and category-coded, in one CSV. Hand it to your CPA in a single share-sheet step.

Ad-Free Premium removes all banners and full-screen ads.

Privacy First

Your books are yours. TaxPot stores your income, deductions and payments on-device with SwiftData. No email signup, no bank linking — we never receive your financial records. The free tier shows ads from our ad partners, and iOS asks your permission before any ad personalization.

Designed For Freelancers

TaxPot isn't a full tax-filing suite. It's the running tally you wish you'd had all year — so when filing season arrives, the math is already done and your records are already clean.

Start logging. Watch your estimate update. Sleep better.
