CashEnvelope

Digital envelopes, kitchen-table simple. CashEnvelope brings the classic cash-stuffing method to your iPhone — no bank link, no account, no analytics. Stuff your paycheck into envelopes for Rent, Groceries, Fun, and Savings, watch the liquid fill bars tick down as you spend, and stay in control of every dollar.

What You Can Do

  • Envelope Wall — every envelope on one screen with live liquid-fill cards (green on track, amber near limit, red over budget)
  • Add Transactions — log spending in two taps, see the wall update instantly
  • Pace Forecast — projects each envelope's end-of-month landing before you actually overspend
  • Roll-Over Behavior — choose roll, reset, or bank-to-savings at month end, per envelope or globally
  • Monthly Overview — pie chart of where your money went, plus trend lines
  • CSV Export — every envelope and every transaction in one shareable file
  • Auto-Allocate Paycheck — define a paycheck template once, tap "Receive Paycheck", and watch every envelope fill automatically by % or fixed amount
  • Daily Reminder — gentle local notification to log yesterday's spending
  • Local Currency — formats automatically to your device locale

Why Cashenvelope Is Different

The big envelope-budgeting apps (YNAB, Goodbudget, EveryDollar, Mvelopes) all want $10–$18 a month, a login, and a bank connection through Plaid. Reddit and the App Store are full of "Plaid disconnected again" complaints and "lost my data when my subscription lapsed" reviews. CashEnvelope skips all of that. No email signup. No bank link. No cloud sync. Your envelopes live on your iPhone, in SwiftData, and nowhere else. If you cancel, your data stays. If you go offline, the app works. If you want privacy, you have it.
