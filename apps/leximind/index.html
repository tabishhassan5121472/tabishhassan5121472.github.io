LexiMind

Vocabulary Builder

LexiMind is a vocabulary builder for people who take words seriously — a real SM-2 spaced-repetition engine, dictionary-quality definitions, and a daily word delivered with proper context, wrapped in a clean iPhone-native shell. No account, no cloud — your word library lives on your device.

How It Works

  • Add words from anything you're reading — books, articles, classes — no deck limits
  • Review with a true SM-2 algorithm, the same spacing method behind Anki
  • Recall Quiz: name the word from its definition, pick the right meaning, or spell it out — every answer updates that word's review schedule
  • Every Daily Word comes with a definition, part of speech, and an example sentence
  • Get a reminder about today's word at the hour you choose
  • Track your streak and see how many reviews are due today

Free Tier

  • Unlimited words, unlimited SM-2 reviews
  • Tag words with your own categories and filter your list by them
  • Light and dark themes
  • Daily reminder notifications

LEXIMIND PREMIUM — $2.99/month, $29.99/year, or $49.99 once for lifetime access:

  • Stats tab — review accuracy, mastered and total-review counts, and a chart of this week's reviews
  • Unlimited Recall Quiz rounds
  • 3D flip review card with on-device audio pronunciation
  • Word Difficulty pools — Casual, Academic, or GRE Prep
  • Export your library as CSV or Markdown
  • Ad-free

Built for anyone closing the gap between abandoned word-of-the-day apps and $10+/month platforms — GRE and exam prep, second-language learners, writers, and readers who want to actually retain what they look up.

No. LexiMind works without an account or sign-in.
