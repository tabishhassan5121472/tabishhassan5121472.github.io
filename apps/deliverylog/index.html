DeliveryLog

Driver Earnings

DeliveryLog tells you what a shift actually paid — after fuel, after mileage, after everything.

Log A Delivery In Seconds

Record the platform, the pay, the tip and the distance. Tips can be added later when they land, so a cash tip at the door does not get lost.

See Real Profit, Not Just Earnings

Deliveries, earnings, tips, expenses and profit for today, this week, this month or any range you pick. Expenses come off the top, so the number you see is the number you kept.

Mileage And Tax

Track distance per delivery and per shift, and get an IRS standard-mileage estimate for the year. Bring a real figure to tax time instead of a guess.

Every Platform In One Place

Work across several apps and see them side by side — which platform actually pays best per hour and per mile, once your costs are in.

Vehicles And Expenses

Track more than one vehicle. Log fuel, maintenance, insurance, tolls and parking, and see them against what you earned.

Goals

Set a daily or weekly target and watch it fill as you work.

On Your Home Screen

A widget keeps today's deliveries, earnings and profit one glance away.

Private By Design

Your delivery records stay on your device. No account, no cloud. Free version shows ads; Premium removes them.

Premium

The free version tracks deliveries and expenses. Premium adds unlimited history, full analytics, multi-vehicle tracking and the tax estimate. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; a one-time lifetime unlock is also available.
