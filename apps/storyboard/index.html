StoryBoard

Story Planner

StoryBoard is the fastest way to plan films, ads, comics, and social videos on iPhone and iPad. Sketch panels with Apple Pencil, drop in reference photos, pick professional camera angles, and export a polished PDF.

What You Can Do

  • Project Management — Create, edit, delete, and duplicate storyboard projects
  • PencilKit Drawing — Full drawing canvas with pen/pencil/marker/eraser (finger + Apple Pencil)
  • Photo Import — Import photos from library via PhotosUI PhotosPicker
  • Camera Angles — 10 professional camera angle presets per panel
  • Templates — 11 predefined templates (film, ad, social media, comic)
  • PDF Export — Professional PDF with panels in 2x3 grid per page
  • Image Grid Export — Single image with all panels via ImageRenderer
  • Aspect Ratios — 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16 support
  • Daily Reminders — Local notification scheduling
  • No account required. Your data is stored on your device, not on a server.
  • Clean, distraction-free design.
  • Your data stays private on your device.

Filmmakers, directors, cinematographers, ad creatives, YouTubers, TikTok creators, animators, comic artists, student film teams, and anyone who plans visual stories before shooting.
