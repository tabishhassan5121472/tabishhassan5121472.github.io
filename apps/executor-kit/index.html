Executor Kit

Settling someone's estate means dozens of tasks, several ledgers and a folder of documents — usually while grieving. Executor Kit keeps all of it in one calm, private place.

A Checklist That Knows What Comes Next

Around 100 executor tasks across seven phases: the first week, who to notify, inventory and valuation, probate administration, debts and taxes, distribution, and closing the estate. Tick tasks off, add your own, set a due date and get a reminder. Mark anything that doesn't apply to this estate as not applicable and it leaves your progress calculation rather than sitting there unfinished forever.

The whole checklist is free and unlimited. It always will be.

Four Ledgers That Add Up

Record the estate's assets, the debts claimed against it, what you have spent out of your own pocket, and what each beneficiary received. The app keeps the running position for you: assets minus debts minus administration costs is what remains to distribute. Debts you have marked disputed are left out of the total until they are settled, so you are never holding money back against a claim that isn't established.

What You Are Owed

Executors routinely pay for funerals, court fees, travel and property upkeep themselves. Flag those as claimable, photograph the receipt, and the app tracks exactly what the estate still owes you — itemised, with the receipts attached, in the report.

One Report, Everything In It

Produce a single PDF: the asset inventory, the debts, the accounting summary, your reimbursement claim with receipt photos, the distribution record, and your checklist progress. Share it with a solicitor, a co-executor or a beneficiary. Premium also exports the same figures as a CSV spreadsheet.

A Document Vault

Photograph the will, the death certificate, the grant of authority, bank statements and correspondence. Lock the whole app behind Face ID or your passcode.

A Fee Estimator

Work out what an executor's compensation would come to from a percentage, an hourly rate, a flat amount, or any combination — and see what that leaves the beneficiaries. Free and unlimited, like the checklist.

Everything Stays On Your Phone

No account. No sign-up. No cloud. Nothing you record is uploaded anywhere, and the app contains no advertising and no tracking.

Free Vs Premium

Free: the entire checklist, the fee estimator, the activity log, 10 assets, 10 debts, 10 expenses, 10 distributions, 5 vault documents, and a watermarked preview of the report. Premium removes every limit and the report watermark. Choose monthly (auto-renewing), yearly (auto-renewing) or a one-time lifetime unlock. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time in your App Store account settings.

Please Note

Executor Kit is an organizer and record-keeper. It is not legal, tax or financial advice, and it is not a substitute for a solicitor, attorney or accountant. Rules for administering an estate differ by country and by region — always verify what applies where you are. What you record here is your own record; no court or authority has reviewed or approved it.
