Penalty Hero

Shootout

Step up to the spot. Penalty Hero is the penalty-shootout game where you play BOTH ends of every duel — first you swipe to shoot, then you switch ends and dive to save. No rigged luck: the physics are fair and learnable, so the better you read the angle, power and curve, the more you score.

Swipe To Shoot

Flick the ball with a swipe. The angle aims it, the length powers it, and a curved swipe bends it around the keeper. The same swipe always produces the same shot — master the curl into the top corner.

Dive To Save

Then the tables turn. Watch the shooter's run-up, read the body lean — honest at lower levels, full of feints against Legends — and dive before the window closes. A real goalkeeper phase — most penalty games skip it entirely.

Daily Challenge

One fixed shootout per day — every player in the world faces the same opponent decisions. Build your streak, beat your personal percentile, come back tomorrow.

Quick Play (free)

Unlimited best-of-5 shootouts against AI keepers across multiple difficulties, with sudden-death tiebreaks. Full shoot-and-save loop, no strings.

48-nation Tournament

Pick a nation and take it through a flag bracket — group stage to the Final, with a fresh draw every run and a trophy room that remembers every campaign. The group stage is free; the knockout rounds and Legend difficulty are part of Pro.

Locker

Unlock ball skins, goalkeeper gloves and stadium backgrounds — from Night Floodlights to Snow, Rain, Sunset, Retro and Rooftop, where the weather even nudges the ball's curve.

Stats & Sharing

Track goals, saves, win rate and your best streak. Share a result card after every shootout.
