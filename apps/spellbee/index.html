SpellBee

Spelling Practice

SpellBee turns spelling practice into something worth coming back to. Hear a word read aloud, type it, and get instant feedback with letter-tile animations. Grade-levelled word banks, a daily challenge and streak tracking keep the habit going.

What You Can Do

  • Practise spelling — hear a word, type it, and see straight away which letters landed
  • Work through grade-levelled banks — hundreds of words organised from Kindergarten to Grade 6
  • Take the daily challenge — ten words a day to build a streak
  • Drill the tricky ones — revisit the words most often misspelled
  • Listen at your own pace — clear pronunciation with slow, normal and fast speeds
  • Track real progress — accuracy over time, words per day, and a breakdown by grade
  • Get a nudge — an optional daily reminder at a time you choose

Free Includes
