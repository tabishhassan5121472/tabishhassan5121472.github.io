EquationBalance

Balance any chemical equation, from a simple H2O synthesis to a full redox reaction, and see the coefficients the moment you tap Balance.

EquationBalance is a pocket chemistry lab for students, teachers and anyone brushing up on the fundamentals. Its solver does real linear algebra: it counts every atom on both sides, reduces the matrix and returns the smallest whole-number coefficients. It understands brackets and nested groups such as Ca(OH)2 and (NH4)2SO4, hydrates such as CuSO4·5H2O, and subscripts and state symbols pasted from a textbook, so you can type the equation exactly as you see it.

The Balancer

  • Type any reaction and get the balanced equation with every coefficient highlighted
  • Handles polyatomic groups, brackets, nested groups and hydrates
  • Clear messages when an equation cannot be balanced, uses an unknown element symbol, or mixes two separate reactions
  • Molar mass shown beside every compound in the result
  • Every balanced equation is saved to your history

Periodic Table

  • All 118 elements in a full table layout with separate lanthanide and actinide rows, colour-coded by category
  • Switch between the table grid and a searchable, filterable list
  • Each element card shows atomic number, symbol, atomic mass, period and group
  • Premium element cards add electron configuration, oxidation states, the discovery story, real-world uses and a "Did you know?" fact

Molar Mass Calculator

  • Enter any formula and get its molar mass in g/mol with a per-element breakdown, brackets and nested groups parsed correctly

Common Reactions

  • A library of 127 classic reactions: synthesis, decomposition, combustion, single and double replacement, acid-base and redox
  • Filter by reaction type and by difficulty, from simple to complex
  • Each reaction comes with an explanation and a real-world example, from airbags to blast furnaces
  • 10 reactions are free; Premium unlocks the full library

Lab Report (premium)

  • Your balancing accuracy, total attempts and days practised
  • The hardest equation you have solved
  • The elements you balance most

Daily Habit

  • Today's chemistry fact on the home screen, featuring a different element
  • A day streak and a running count of equations balanced
  • Optional daily notification with a chemistry fact, at the hour you choose

Lab Notebook Backup (optional)

Sign in with Apple or Google to back up your saved equations, balancing history and practice results, then restore them on a new device. Sign-in is optional: the balancer, periodic table and calculator all work without an account, and your equations and history are uploaded only if you choose to back them up. Anonymous crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics so bugs can be fixed. Settings > Delete Account removes the account, the backup and the data on your device.

No ads, ever. Light and dark mode, iPhone and iPad, portrait and landscape.
