ParchmentBox

Highlight Saver

ParchmentBox is a quiet home for the passages that move you. Paste a quote from a book, article, podcast, tweet, or conversation, tag it, and it becomes part of a private library that resurfaces itself — one highlight a day, right when you want to see it again.

No account. No sync servers. No feed. Everything lives on your device.

What You Can Do

  • Paste any passage and save it in seconds — pick a source template (Book, Article, Podcast, Tweet, Conversation) so every highlight is properly attributed
  • Tag highlights and browse your whole library by tag
  • Search across every saved highlight and book
  • Get one Daily Resurface notification at the hour you choose, showing a random highlight from your own library
  • Turn any highlight into a shareable image with the Classic Parchment card theme
  • Find your saved highlights right from iOS Spotlight search
  • Fully offline — your quotes never leave your iPhone

PARCHMENTBOX PREMIUM — $2.99/month, $29.99/year, or $49.99 once for lifetime access:

  • Multiple resurfaces per day, on your schedule — not just one
  • Unlimited tags with custom tag colors
  • Markdown export of your full library or highlights grouped by book, via the share sheet
  • Source Templates for Book, Article, Podcast, Tweet, and Conversation auto-prefill
  • Three additional share-card themes — Sepia Vellum, Midnight Ink, and Bookmark Red
  • Ad-free experience

Perfect For

Readers, students, and anyone who underlines a good line and never sees it again. ParchmentBox is built for people who want their own highlights back in front of them, not someone else's algorithm.
