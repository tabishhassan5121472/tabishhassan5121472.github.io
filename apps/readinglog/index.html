ReadingLog

Book Tracker

ReadingLog is a beautiful personal book tracker for avid readers. Search Open Library to add any book instantly, time your reading sessions, save notes and favorite quotes, and visualize your yearly reading journey through elegant charts.

What You Can Do

  • Library Management — Add books manually or search Open Library API
  • Reading Sessions — Timer-based tracking with page progress
  • Notes & Quotes — Save notes and favorite quotes per book
  • Reading Statistics — Swift Charts: books/month, pages/month, time trends
  • Reading Goals — Annual book target with progress tracking
  • Premium IAP — Monthly subscription (free=10 books limit, premium=unlimited)
  • Daily Reminders — Local notifications for reading reminders
  • Haptics — Feedback on all interactive elements
  • Open Library API — Online book search with cover images
  • No sign-up and no account — your library, notes and reading sessions stay on your device.
  • A quiet reading room, not a feed: no social timeline and no streak nagging, just your books.
  • Your data stays private on your device.

READINGLOG PREMIUM — $2.99/month:

  • Unlimited books (free tier: 10 books)
  • Notes and quotes for every book
  • Annual reading goals
  • Reading statistics and trends
  • Open Library cover search

Perfect For

Book lovers, bibliophiles, students, book club members, and anyone who wants to remember every book they've read and every quote worth keeping.

Choose the plan that suits you: monthly, yearly, or a one-time Lifetime purchase that never renews.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple ID subscription settings. No. ReadingLog works without an account or sign-in.

ReadingLog runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17.0 or newer.
