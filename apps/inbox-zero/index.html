Inbox Zero

Email Sorter

Inbox Zero — Email Sorter is a reply-time tracker for busy professionals. Log incoming emails, set response targets per priority, snooze for follow-up, and watch your Inbox Score climb as you reply on time.

What You Can Do

  • Track every pending email with priority, age, and missed-target badges
  • Set per-priority response windows (Urgent / High / Medium / Low) with on-time reminders
  • Snooze emails into a Follow-Up list grouped Overdue / Today / Later
  • Build auto-sorting rules that classify new emails by keyword
  • Watch your weekly Inbox Score with a four-quadrant breakdown
  • Smart tips appear when your backlog grows or targets are missed
  • No account, no email sync — your data stays on your device

Free Plan

  • 20 emails tracked per week
  • 4 starter sorting rules
  • 7-day statistics history
  • Core response targets and Follow-Up list

INBOX ZERO PREMIUM — $2.99/month:

  • Unlimited email tracking (no weekly cap)
  • Unlimited sorting rules
  • Custom email categories
  • Full Inbox Score breakdown with per-priority on-time rate
  • All-time reply-time charts and category trends
  • CSV export of your full history
  • Ad-free experience

Perfect For

Busy professionals, remote workers, freelancers, customer-success managers, founders, and anyone who needs to reply on time without missing follow-ups.
