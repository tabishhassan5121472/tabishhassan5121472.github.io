TeachKit

Teacher Toolkit

TeachKit is the all-in-one toolkit that helps teachers run their classroom from a single, beautifully simple app. Take attendance in seconds, keep a clean gradebook, track homework, plan your timetable, log behavior, and turn it all into clear reports and charts — no spreadsheets, no clutter.

Built for real classrooms and designed to work fully offline, TeachKit keeps your students' data private and right at your fingertips.

What You Can Do

Attendance

  • Mark present, absent, late, or excused with a single tap
  • Mark the whole class present or absent at once
  • See attendance rates per student and per class at a glance
  • Spot patterns before they become problems

Gradebook

  • Record scores for assignments, quizzes, and exams
  • Weighted assignments and automatic averages
  • Exclude diagnostics so practice work never drags a grade down
  • Watch each student's progress build over the term

Homework & Assignments

  • Create assignments with due dates and track who has turned them in
  • Attach photos of the work set
  • Quick completion overview for the whole class

Reminders

  • A morning summary of the day ahead
  • Homework due-date reminders, however many days of notice you want
  • A heads-up before each class on your timetable
  • All scheduled on your device — nothing is sent to a server

Timetable

  • Build your weekly schedule and see your day at a glance
  • Keep classes, rooms, and periods organized

Behavior Log

  • Record positive notes and incidents with context
  • Build a fair, documented picture of each student

Parent Messages

  • Prefilled templates for absences, grade updates, and behavior notes
  • Send by email or text through your own Mail and Messages apps
  • Bulk messages go out blind — parents never see each other's contact details

Classroom Tools

  • Random student picker, group generator, and seating charts
  • Countdown, Pomodoro, and stopwatch timers

Insights & Reports

  • Visual charts for attendance, grades, and behavior trends
  • At-risk student flags so nobody slips through
  • Class and student summaries you can review anytime

Your data stays on your device unless you turn on cloud sync. TeachKit works offline and never sells your information.
