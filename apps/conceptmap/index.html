ConceptMap

Mind Mapper

ConceptMap turns a blank canvas into a mind map in seconds — brainstorm a project, outline an essay, or break a big decision into branches, all with a drag-and-connect canvas that stays on your device. No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync required.

Start Fast With Templates

  • Brainstorm Starter template gets you a root node with four branches instantly
  • Full canvas editor — drag nodes, draw connections, recolor branches, two zoom levels
  • Snap-to-grid alignment keeps every map tidy
  • Export any map to PNG, watermark-free
  • Apple Shortcuts: "Open Last Map" and "New ConceptMap" from Siri or the Shortcuts app
  • Build up to 3 maps with up to 20 nodes each — enough for a real workflow

Conceptmap Premium

  • Unlimited maps and unlimited nodes per map
  • 6 additional templates: SWOT Analysis, Project Plan, Decision Tree, Goal & OKRs, Study Map, and Pros & Cons
  • Auto-Layout Engine — organic, radial, and tree arrangements at one tap
  • Node Style Templates — Rounded, Pill, Card, and Circle shapes with a default color
  • Quiz Mode — test your recall of any node's children, great for exam prep
  • Ad-free canvas

Premium is available as a $2.99/month subscription, a $29.99/year subscription, or a $49.99 one-time Lifetime purchase that unlocks everything permanently.

Private By Design

Every map you build is stored on your device with SwiftData — nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to create or lose. ConceptMap is built for students, project planners, and anyone who thinks better when ideas branch out visually instead of living in a bulleted list.
