FamilyDinner

Meal Planner

Dinner is the one time the whole family is in the same place. FamilyDinner helps you make it count — decide what's on the table, see who's home, and always have something better to talk about than "how was school?"

No account. No sign-up. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What's On The Table

Pick tonight's meal from your own saved list. FamilyDinner remembers when you last served each one, so you can stop making the same three dinners on repeat.

Who's At The Table

Add everyone in the family and mark who's home tonight. A quick tap tells you how many places to set.

Conversation Starters

A different question at every dinner, drawn from a library of over 150 prompts written for real families — not corporate icebreakers. Shuffle for a new one any time. Turn on Kid-Friendly Prompts Only when little ones are at the table.

Categories include Childhood, Future Plans, Hypotheticals, Gratitude, Quick Wins and Storytelling. You can write your own prompts too.

Free Includes

  • Who's-at-the-table tracker
  • The first 30 conversation starters

Private By Design

Your meals, your family names and your prompts are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData. We never upload them. There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
