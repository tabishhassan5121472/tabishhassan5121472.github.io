MatchPool

Soccer Picks

MatchPool turns the summer 2026 soccer tournament into a friendly prediction league. Create a pool, share a 6-character join code, and everyone predicts the scoreline for every match. The app does the maths and ranks your group on a live leaderboard — all you have to do is call it right.

This is a game for fun and bragging rights. There is no real-money wagering, no betting, no odds and no prizes of any kind.

Why Matchpool

  • See exactly how you score — tap any match for a full points breakdown (exact score, correct

margin, correct result). No mystery points.

  • Works offline — all 104 fixtures are bundled, with kickoff times and venues.
  • One code to join. No accounts, no email, no sign-up friction for your friends.
  • Predictions lock automatically at kickoff, so nobody can edit after the whistle.
  • Syncs with friends through iCloud, or run a local pass-the-phone pool on one device.

Free

  • Create a pool (up to 10 members) and predict all 104 matches
  • Standard 5-3-2 scoring with the full points breakdown
  • Live leaderboard, round-by-round standings and shareable invite + standings cards
  • Joining any pool is always free

Matchpool Pro

  • Unlimited pools — run your office, family and friends leagues at once
  • Bigger pools (up to 50 members)
  • Build your own scoring rules: point weights, knockout multipliers and a per-round joker

double-down pick

  • Deep rivalry stats — head-to-head records, accuracy profiles, streaks and biggest upsets

MatchPool Pro is $2.99/month and starts with a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings.
