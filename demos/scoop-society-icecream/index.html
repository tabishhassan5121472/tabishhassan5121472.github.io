SCOOP SOCIETY is a small, independent parlour concept built around one idea: the best part of ice cream is the bit you hand across the table. We churn in small batches, keep the wall short enough to taste properly, and let the colours do the talking.

◐

Small batches

A short wall we can churn properly. When a tub runs out, it runs out, and something else takes its place.

✦

Colour you can taste

Pistachio looks like pistachio. Pink comes from strawberries. If the colour is loud, the flavour earned it.

◡

Always a spare spoon

Ask for a taste before you choose. Ask for a second spoon when you do.

Good questions.

Can I taste a flavour before choosing?+

Yes. Ask at the counter for a tasting spoon of anything in the cabinet.

Is anything allergen-free?+

We list the allergens in each sample recipe, but scoops, cones and toppings share a counter and equipment. We cannot promise any scoop is free from an allergen. Please ask the team before ordering.

Are the sorbets dairy-free?+

The sorbet recipes are made without milk, but they are scooped from the same cabinet as dairy flavours. Ask us to use a clean scoop.

Can I order for a party?+

Use the parlour enquiry below. This is a demonstration site, so nothing is sent and no order is placed.

Something on your mind?

Flavour questions, allergy questions, a birthday for twelve. Write us a note. This demonstration saves the draft in your browser and sends nothing.
