Procurement questions

Which tolerances can you hold?+

This page does not publish machine capability figures. Tolerances are taken from your drawing and confirmed or questioned at contract review, before a price is given.

Can I upload drawings here?+

Not on this demo site. There is no secure file storage behind the form, so it does not take uploads. Quote the drawing number and revision. A live supplier would ask for the files through a secure channel.

Do you hold ISO 9001 or other certification?+

No certification is claimed by this demonstration brand. Always ask a real supplier for current certificates and check their scope.

What is the difference between fixed and configurable parts?+

Fixed catalogue items have one set of sample drawing values. Configurable parts let you pick values within stated ranges, and invalid combinations are blocked before they reach the RFQ.
