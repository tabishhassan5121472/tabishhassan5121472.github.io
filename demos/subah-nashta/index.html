Breakfast is the meal we share first.

Before you come

Is breakfast served all day?+

No. Breakfast runs from opening until 12:00 on weekdays, 11:30 on Friday and 12:30 at weekends. These are the illustrative demo hours shown above.

When is halwa puri available?+

The halwa puri thaal is a Friday to Sunday dish in this demo menu. Every other combo is available whenever the kitchen is open.

What is the difference between a combo and a single item?+

Combos are fixed-price plates with set slots you choose within. Single items are priced on their own and can be added in any quantity.

Can you cater for allergies?+

Dish descriptions list main ingredients only, not a complete allergen list. Please speak to the team about ingredients and kitchen handling before ordering.
