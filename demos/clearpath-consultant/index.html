How the method plays out.

Each file follows the same structure: context, problem, the work performed and what was documented at close.

Case file 01

Context
A design and engineering consultancy, about 40 people, two offices.
Engagement
Diagnostic Sprint · 6 weeks

Composite illustration. Written for this demonstration from typical engagements. Not a client record, and not evidence of results; names and identifying details are deliberately absent.

From a stalled handoff to a single intake brief.

The problem

Approved projects waited weeks before kickoff. Delivery leads re-asked clients for information already given during the sale, and the firm could not say where projects were waiting.

Work performed

Traced three recent projects from first call to kickoff with the people involved. Mapped fourteen handoff points, marked missing inputs and unclear owners, then redesigned the intake with sales and delivery leads in two working sessions.

Documented at close

  • One intake brief replaced three partial documents.
  • Each of five remaining handoffs was given a named owner.
  • A weekly fifteen-minute review of work waiting on a decision was put on the calendar and run by the operations lead.

The one-page decision brief.

Seven prompts we use before any leadership meeting that must end in a decision. Plain text — copy it into whatever your team already uses.

  1. The decision, as a question
  2. Why now
  3. Criteria, agreed first
  4. Options, including “do nothing”
  5. Evidence for and against
  6. Recommendation and stop conditions
  7. Who to consult and inform

We ask only for what we need to prepare. Please do not include confidential financials or personal information about colleagues. In this demonstration your brief is stored only in this browser and is never sent.
