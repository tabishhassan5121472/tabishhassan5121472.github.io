What goes in the glass.

Whole fruit, cut and pressed for each order. Here is what we use and how we use it, in plain words.

How a glass gets made.

Four steps, done for each order.

01

Wash and cut

Fruit is washed and cut through the day in small batches.

02

Press or blend

Juices go through the press; smoothies go in the blender with ice. Each drink is made when you order it.

03

Pour and label

Your cup gets a sticker with the blend name, size and any changes you asked for.

04

Drink it cold

Fresh juice tastes best straight away, so we do not make drinks ahead of time.

Good to know.

Are ingredients listed?+

Every drink lists its main ingredients. Smoothies with milk or yoghurt say so. A live menu would publish full allergen information.

Do the drinks have health benefits?+

We do not make health claims. These are fresh fruit drinks, made to taste good.

Can I change my drink after adding it?+

Yes. Change the size, quantity or remove a drink in your order before checkout.

What sizes are there?+

Regular is 300 ml and Large is 450 ml. Large costs Rs. 120 more.

Are seasonal drinks always available?+

No. They are on while the fruit is in season. This demo does not show dates because none are fixed.

Drinks for a crowd?

Office mornings, birthdays, a stall at a school fair. Tell us what you need and we’ll draft a plan. This demo saves your draft in this browser and does not send anything.
