Pressed hard.Built fast.

STACKED is a short-menu burger counter. We smash, crumb and cut in-house, then build everything to order.

01

Smashed, not stacked high on hype

Every beef patty is pressed thin onto a hot flat-top so the edge goes crisp while the middle stays juicy.

02

Cut and crumbed in-house

Fries are cut from whole potatoes each day; chicken is marinated in buttermilk and crumbed to order.

03

A short menu on purpose

Six categories, one builder, four combos. Fewer things, done the same way every time.

Good to know

Can I customise my meal?+

Every burger can be made a meal, and most items have a quick choice such as pickles, heat, dip or size. For full control, use the stack builder. Your choices stay with each item in the basket.

Is delivery available everywhere?+

No. This demo shows an example delivery area of about 5 km around the kitchen, with an illustrative Rs. 150 fee. Real zones and fees would be confirmed at checkout.

Do you have allergen information?+

Each item lists its main allergens as sample information. All fried items share fryers. Please ask the counter before ordering if you have an allergy.

Can I track my order live?+

Not on this site. There is no live tracking integration. After checkout you get a reference and an example status, clearly marked as a demo.

Feeding a crowd?

Party boxes, office lunches or a long table for the squad. Tell us the plan and we will draft the order. This demo saves your draft in this browser only.
